Assistive technology concept

DotLearn 20

A refreshable braille tutor for visually impaired students. It teaches letters one at a time, turns class materials into braille and speech, and lets students write with a six-key keyboard, so they can learn and study on their own.

Try the display

Pin clicks, key taps and a spoken voice.

The letter appears in the first cell, with a practice word after it. The matching keys on the keyboard light up, so students learn to read and write the letter together.

A short reading passage flows across the 20 cells one line at a time, the way a student pans through a textbook.
